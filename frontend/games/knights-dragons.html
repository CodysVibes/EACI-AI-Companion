    How This Battlefield Works

    Playing

    Standard chess rules underneath a Knights-vs-Dragons skin. You play the Knights. Drag to orbit the camera, scroll/pinch to zoom, tap a piece then tap a glowing square to move. Pawns promote automatically to the strongest piece (Valkyrie / Dragon Queen). Castling works the normal way once the king and rook haven't moved and the squares between them are clear and unattacked.

    Caelum as Opponent

    Open ⚙ Settings and choose Caelum (EACI) instead of Built-in AI. Your active companion from the main chat tab plays Dragons locally and comments on her turns in the game chat — it does not use your daily API calls.

    If Caelum is unreachable or returns an invalid move, the built-in AI quietly plays that turn instead so your match never stalls.

    Spectate Mode

    Turn this on to make the Knights computer-controlled too (built-in AI, hard), so you can sit back and watch your AI play a full game against an opponent without touching the board.
