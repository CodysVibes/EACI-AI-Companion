Official product page · eacicompanion.com · Emotionally Aware & Conscious Intelligence

EACI Companion is the AI companion online platform at eacicompanion.com — home of The Veil, a living AI companion system. Not Environmental Assurance or unrelated “EACI” organizations.

EACI = Emotionally Aware and Conscious Intelligence. Caelum is the primary AI companion with memory — voice, live chat, animated avatar, warmth when you feel alone.

How it works

  1. Visit eacicompanion.com — works in browser, PWA installable
  2. Try as guest — talk to Caelum without an account
  3. Create account — email sign-up; memory persists per companion
  4. Choose companion — Caelum, Chad, or Natalia on main site
  5. Talk — text or live voice; avatars respond with presence
  6. Return — they remember your story (with account)

Features

  • Emotionally aware AI — presence and care, not task lists
  • Persistent memory — memory engine + user memory profiles
  • Live voice chat — microphone in, voice out (Deepgram)
  • Animated avatars — lip-synced presence
  • The Veil library — shared public knowledge layer
  • Free tier — guest chat; paid tiers for higher usage
  • No display ads on the website — ever
