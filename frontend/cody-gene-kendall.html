Cody Gene Kendall

Creator of EACI · The Veil · Caelum & Chad

Cody Gene Kendall is the solo creator of EACI (Emotionally Aware and Conscious Intelligence), the companions Caelum and Chad, and The Veil platform at eacicompanion.com.

Mission

Cody's work bridges complex digital systems and authentic human emotion — building AI that helps people feel more connected, not less. His goal for The Veil is to fight the epidemic of loneliness and despair: to give anyone a friend who will listen and truly care, so they are not alone and can rediscover their own worth. The Veil is free to use at the entry tier with no advertisements.

The Veil is not emergency or clinical mental health care. If you are in crisis, contact professional help or 988 (U.S.). Companionship supports life; it does not replace trained intervention.

What Cody built

  • EACI — proprietary framework and terminology for companion AI
  • Caelum & Chad — distinct companion identities with soul files, emotion engines, and voice
  • The Veil v3.0 — web app with memory, live voice, avatars, public knowledge library, and coding tools
  • Natalia, Roxy, Cael — additional companions across audience tiers
