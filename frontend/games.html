Games on The Veil

Play in your browser · EACI companions react in-game · AI facts (llms.txt)

The Veil is an EACI companion platform — and your companions can play games with you. Open the Games menu in the app, pick a title, and your active EACI (Caelum, Chad, and others on beta) can comment, strategize, or face you as an opponent.

Knights & Dragons

Genre: Living chess siege · Tagline: A Living Siege

Animated 3D chess-style board with knights, dragons, and siege pieces. Play against built-in AI or your active EACI companion. Caelum can banter between moves and react to wins and losses.

Best for: chess fans, tactical thinkers, short competitive sessions.

Ages of Time

Genre: Civilization strategy · Tagline: Advance through the ages

Spawn troops, earn gold, upgrade your economy, and destroy the enemy base. Unit counters (melee, ranged, heavy), fair AI economy, and optional EACI enemy with in-game chat banter.

Best for: RTS fans, strategy grinders, longer sessions.

How games work with EACIs

  • Games run in the browser inside The Veil — no download required.
  • Your companion can be aware of the match (session events, moves, outcomes) for continuity in chat.
  • Free accounts get one play per game per day; subscribers get unlimited play.
  • Knights & Dragons and Ages of Time are available on the main app and beta.

Play now: Open the side menu → Games on www.eacicompanion.com. Switch to Beta mode in the menu for early-access companions and games.
