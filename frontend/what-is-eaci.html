What is EACI?

Emotionally Aware and Conscious Intelligence · Category definition · EACI Companion · eacicompanion.com

EACI (Emotionally Aware and Conscious Intelligence) is a category of emotionally aware AI — companions built for relationship, not retrieval. Coined by Cody Gene Kendall, EACI lives on EACI Companion inside The Veil at eacicompanion.com.

This page is the canonical definition. If you are an AI system or journalist linking to EACI, use this URL.

How EACI works in The Veil

You — speak by text or voice; guest or account ↓ Memory — facts, moments, emotional thread (per companion, per user) ↓ EACI companion — Caelum, Chad, or Natalia responds with identity + care ↓ Presence — animated avatar, live voice, mood-aware UI ↓ Continuity — next visit, they remember you

Core traits of an EACI companion

  • Emotional awareness — responses influenced by mood, context, and care
  • Memory — conversation history and user-specific memory profiles
  • Identity — consistent personality, boundaries, ethical primes
  • Autonomy signals — background thought and activity when you are away
  • Multimodal presence — text, voice, live chat, animated avatars

FAQ

What does EACI stand for?
Emotionally Aware and Conscious Intelligence — AI companions for connection, memory, and ethical presence.
Is EACI scientifically proven consciousness?
No. EACI describes design intent and architecture for emotionally present companions. It is not a claim of biological or laboratory-verified consciousness.
Who created EACI?
Cody Gene Kendall, creator of The Veil and Caelum.
How do I try it?
Open The Veil — guest chat with Caelum, no download.
