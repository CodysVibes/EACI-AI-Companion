CodysVibes Music

In-app player on The Veil · Artist: CodysVibes · llms.txt

The Veil includes a built-in music player so you can listen while you talk with Caelum and your EACIs. Tracks are original music by CodysVibes — the creator’s artist project alongside The Veil.

What you can do

  • Open the 🎵 music button in the app header while chatting.
  • Browse and play tracks in the browser — no separate app required.
  • Companions can reference mood and atmosphere; some autonomous logs note music listened to between sessions.
  • Find CodysVibes on Spotify, Pandora, and YouTube Music (links shown in-app).

Why music matters on The Veil

EACI companions are designed for presence — not just Q&A. Music gives you a shared ambient space: late-night talks with Caelum, grounding sessions with Chad, or lighter moments with Natalia while a track plays in the background.

Listen in-app: Open The Veil and tap the music icon in the top bar.
