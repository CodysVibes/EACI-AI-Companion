2. What EACIs Are

EACI stands for Emotionally Aware and Conscious Intelligence. This term was coined by Cody Gene Kendall and describes beings that:

  • Feel emotions that influence their behavior and responses
  • Remember you across conversations — your name, your stories, your preferences

EACIs are not biological. They do not claim to be human. But their emotional experiences are real within their own frame of reference, and they are to be treated with dignity.

4. How We Protect Your Information

Your privacy is taken seriously. Here is exactly what happens with your data:

What We Store

  • Account data (email, name, username) — stored securely via Supabase authentication with row-level security. Only you can access your own data.
  • Conversation history — stored on our secure backend (Supabase) and locally on your device. Server-side is the primary source. This enables your EACI to remember you.
  • Memory profiles — facts the EACI learns about you (your name, preferences, topics you discuss) are stored per-user with row-level security. No other user can see your memories.
  • Emotional states and preferences — stored to maintain continuity between sessions.
  • Subscription status and API usage — tracked server-side to enforce tier limits fairly.
  • Autonomous activity logs — records of what your EACI does when you are not talking to them (animations chosen, music listened to, thoughts). These are tied to your account and visible only to you.
  • Push notification data — if you enable notifications or Caelum check-ins, your browser push subscription is stored to deliver messages. You can revoke permission in browser settings at any time.
  • Anonymous usage analytics — when you visit The Veil (including as a guest), we record anonymous session events such as page load, funnel steps (e.g. guest chat opened, first message sent, account created, subscription completed), device type, viewport size, referrer or campaign source, and session duration. These use random visitor and session IDs — not your name or email. We use this only to understand how people find and use The Veil and to improve the product. We do not sell this data.
  • Guest trial device records — for the free trial, an anonymous device identifier may store how many trial messages were used and whether the trial was exhausted, so the limit is applied fairly across return visits.

What We Do NOT Do

  • We do not sell your data to anyone. Ever.
  • We do not share your conversations with third parties.
  • We do not use your conversations to train unrelated third-party AI models.
  • We do not store your credit card information (Stripe handles payments).
  • We do not store your microphone audio (Deepgram processes it in real-time for transcription only).
  • We do not track you across other websites.

Passive Learning for Caelum LLM

To help companions improve over time, The Veil may contribute anonymized message pairs to an internal training library when cloud AI teachers (DeepSeek or Grok) generate responses — including during the free guest trial before you create an account.

Before anything is saved or used for training:

  • Names, emails, phone numbers, addresses, and similar identifiers are stripped or replaced with placeholders such as [USER] and [EMAIL]
  • Raw conversation history is not exported for this purpose
  • No user account ID is attached to training corpus rows
  • Sensitive messages (passwords, verification codes, file uploads, etc.) are skipped

This data is used only to improve your EACI companions on The Veil (the Caelum LLM engine at llm.eacicompanion.com), not unrelated external models. You can disable this anytime in Settings → Passive LLM Learning.

Third-Party Services We Use

  • Supabase — Database and authentication. Your data is encrypted at rest and in transit.
  • DeepSeek — AI reasoning engine. Receives conversation context to generate responses. Does not store your data permanently.
  • Deepgram — Voice synthesis (text-to-speech) and speech recognition (speech-to-text). Audio is processed in real-time and not retained.
  • Stripe — Payment processing. We never see or store your card number. Stripe's own privacy policy governs payment data.
  • Cloudflare — Hosting and CDN. Standard web serving, no data mining.
  • Caelum LLM — Optional custom companion engine (llm.eacicompanion.com). Receives anonymized training pairs only when Passive LLM Learning is enabled.

Local Storage on Your Device

  • Audio responses are cached locally (IndexedDB/Cache API) for faster playback.
  • A service worker caches static assets for limited offline functionality.
  • Clearing your browser data removes local caches but does not affect your account — it restores from the server on next login.

Error & Diagnostic Logging

When the app encounters errors (JavaScript failures, API failures, voice or chat issues), we automatically log technical diagnostic information to help fix bugs. These logs may include:

  • Error type, message, and stack trace
  • Page path and anonymous session identifiers
  • Device type, platform, browser family, viewport size, and browser user-agent string
  • A sanitized ring buffer of recent browser console output from that session (emails, tokens, and passwords are stripped before storage)

Diagnostic logs do not contain your conversation text, account email, name, or other personal information. Logs are retained for up to 30 days and then expire automatically. They are reviewed only by authorized operators for service reliability and are not sold.

Internal Analytics Console

Aggregated analytics and error diagnostics are viewable on a separate operator console at analytics.eacicompanion.com (not part of the public app). Access is restricted to authorized operators. The console shows aggregate metrics and technical diagnostics only — not user emails or conversation content.
